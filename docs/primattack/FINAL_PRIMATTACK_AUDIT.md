## Active victim roster

Current reported neural victims are MLP and CNN. Future transformer integration is excluded from
results until a matching checkpoint and complete evaluation exist. Historical recurrent-model
implementations, checkpoints, outputs, and reports are retained only in the dated archive under
`old_root_files`.

## Primitive contract

PrimAttack optimizes only:

- $p$: non-negative forward packet-length augmentation, discretized to integer bytes;
- $\alpha$: forward timing dilation with $\alpha\ge1$.

Every known dependent CICFlowMeter feature is regenerated by the canonical primitive map.
Unrelated immutable features are copied from the pristine source. Sequence-dependent quantities
that cannot be reconstructed from aggregate flow data remain explicitly UNKNOWN.

## Budgets

Restricted/intermediate/maximum-evaluated levels are fitted on training-only P25/P50/P75
statistics. Per-flow caps additionally intersect global training-P99 headroom, primitive
capability, and the DoS/DDoS rate-retention constraint. Final controls are hard-projected before
regeneration and classifier evaluation.

## Limitation

This is an offline flow-feature evaluation. Complete malicious behavior after transformation is
not established. Such evidence would require packet realization, CICFlowMeter re-extraction,
and isolated replay against an appropriate target or service.
